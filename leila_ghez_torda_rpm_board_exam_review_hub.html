Target Passing Score Rule (RA 10029)

To pass the Psychometrician Licensure Examination, an examinee must obtain a weighted general average of at least 75% across all four subjects, with no grade lower than 60% in any given subject. (And our Cum Laude is aiming for Topnotcher! 🌟)

Special BLEPP Tip: The 3 Good Moral Certificates!

Unlike other board exams, PRC BLEPP strictly requires three (3) separate Certificates of Good Moral Character from any of the following:

  • School / University: From National University MOA (Student Affairs / Dean's Office)
  • Barangay: Barangay Good Moral / Clearance where she resides
  • Church / Parish OR Employer: Signed by the parish priest/pastor or current employer
